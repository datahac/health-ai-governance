# health-ai-governance
Anonymised supplementary materials for a longitudinal study of responsible AI governance across 21 health AI innovation projects.

#  Responsible AI Governance in Health AI Innovation

This repository provides supplementary research materials for an anonymised academic submission examining how resource-constrained Health AI projects operationalise Responsible AI (RAI) governance across organisational boundaries.

The study draws on longitudinal records from 21 UK and European Health AI innovation projects. It identifies governance episodes within everyday project work and compares recurring governance tasks, their initiating conditions, participating actors and situated responses.

The supplementary materials document the analytical framework, coding approach and cross-project comparison procedure. Confidential project records and personally identifiable information are not made publicly available.
